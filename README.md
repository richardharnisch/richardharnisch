# Hi, I'm Richard 👋

I'm an AI founder and researcher based in Amsterdam. I'm in the MSc Artificial Intelligence and a research assistant at the University of Amsterdam, where I work with Dr. Shashikant Ilager on energy-efficient AI data-center design in the Multiscale Networked Systems Group. I also founded [Derylus](https://derylus.com).

I'm especially interested in optimization, reinforcement learning, agents, and reliable LLM systems. I also have industry ML experience from projects with [Gasunie](https://www.gasunie.nl/) and [descript](https://descript.de/).

## Selected work

### 🥇 [Derylus](https://derylus.com): Up to 4x speedup on edge model inference

First place at the Tulip MVP Sprint and now its own startup. We make your model run up up to 4x faster on your silicon by using ChipHappens, our agentic loop-based optimizer. Code is not public, unfortunately. If you or anyone you know is deploying any models on edge hardware, please reach out to [founders@derylus.com](mailto:founders@derylus.com)!

### 🥇 [Maverx Style Buddy](https://github.com/richardharnisch/maverx-style-buddy): agentic slide generation

First place at the Digilize AI Hackathon 2026. The system learns a visual style from existing presentations, then generates new, fully editable PowerPoint decks and supporting materials. My work covered the prompts, agent architecture, product framing, demo, and pitch.

### 🔬 [UvA Research Assistant](https://github.com/Large-scale-Sustainable-Computing-LSC): Data Center Efficiency

Working as a part-time Research Assistant at the University of Amsterdam on efficient data center job schedulers that leverage electricity price forecasts to make deadline-aware work schedules while minimizing electricity cost and carbon intensity. Targeting publication at ACM e-Energy 2027.

### 👷 [Internship at Gasunie](https://github.com/kodeInInk/PriceForecasting-GasuniexRUG): Day-ahead electricity price forecasting

Worked in a five-person team on forecasting Dutch electricity prices for Gasunie, with a focus on evaluation, retraining, and distribution shift rather than only in-sample model performance.
